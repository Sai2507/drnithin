About Us
Globally trained orthopaedic surgeon, specializing in sports injuries, arthroscopy, shoulder, knee, and robotic surgeries.
Dr. Nithin Kumar
Bejjanki
MRCS(UK), FRCS(Tr&Ortho), MCh Ortho, DSEM(International Olympic Committee).
Fellowship shoulder(UK), Fellowship Knee (UK).
Dr. Nithin is accomplished Orthopaedic Surgeon with expertise in Complex Shoulder Injuries, Complex Knee Injuries, Advanced Arthroscopy surgeries, Shoulder replacements, Knee realignment & Latest Cartilage Regeneration.
Considered the most Ethical and skillfull Surgeon, Dr Nithin Kumar Bejjanki is an outstanding Orthopaedic surgeon, who has specialised in Arthroscopy and Sports Injuries. He has treated many International sports professionals and helped them in winning medals for the country.
Because of his interest in teaching, Dr Nithin is an accomplished speaker in national conferences, author of books and also heads the fellowship programme to train junior surgeons in Arthroscopy.
His surgical training was carried out at the renowned centres in Birmingham, Sheffield, Yorkshire, and finally did fellowship in London. He did shoulder fellowship in prestigious Calvert Fellowship under world renowned Dr Andrew Wallace for shoulders and Dr Andy Williams in Knee.
Dr Nithin works extensively with a number of charities providing his surgical services and helping develop the surgical skills and adoption of new technology.
Health and fitness play a big part in Dr Nithin’s leisure time. He is a former national Lawn tennis player.
Guiding Path to Exceptional Care
Mission
To deliver ethical, advanced, and compassionate orthopedic care, specializing in arthroscopy and sports injuries, restoring mobility, enhancing performance, and empowering patients to achieve healthier, active, and fulfilling lives.

To be a globally recognized leader in orthopedic excellence, pioneering innovation, education, and research while shaping future surgeons and transforming patient outcomes through world-class surgical expertise and compassionate care.
Values
Ethics, integrity, compassion, innovation, excellence in surgical practice, continuous learning, commitment to patient recovery, and dedication to advancing orthopedic care for individuals, athletes, and the broader medical community.
