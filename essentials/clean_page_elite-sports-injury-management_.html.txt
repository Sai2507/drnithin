Elite Sports Injury Management
Elite Sports Injury Management is a highly specialized service designed for athletes, fitness enthusiasts, and active individuals who demand peak physical performance. Sports injuries not only affect mobility but can also interrupt professional careers, training schedules, and long-term athletic goals. At the forefront of this field, Dr. Nithin Kumar Bejjanki combines advanced orthopedic techniques with personalized rehabilitation strategies to help athletes recover quickly and return to their sport stronger than before. His unique experience as a former national lawn tennis player gives him an in-depth understanding of both the physical and psychological demands athletes face during recovery.

Sports injuries vary widely depending on the intensity of training, type of sport, and individual biomechanics. Common conditions managed under this service include:
Ligament tears such as ACL, PCL, and ankle ligament injuries.
Meniscus injuries caused by twisting or high-impact movements.
Cartilage injuries that can impair joint health and performance.
Shoulder and knee dislocations , often common in contact sports.
Rotator cuff tears , leading to pain and restricted movement.
Overuse injuries like tendinitis and stress fractures.
Chronic pain and swelling that affect training and competition.
By addressing both acute injuries and long-term wear and tear, Dr. Nithin ensures athletes receive comprehensive care.
The focus of Elite Sports Injury Management is precision, innovation, and faster recovery. Treatments include:
Minimally invasive arthroscopy for knee, shoulder, ankle, and elbow injuries.
ACL reconstruction and revision surgeries for restoring stability and function.
Meniscus repair and preservation techniques to protect joint health.
Cartilage repair and regeneration surgery using modern biologics.
Robotic-assisted orthopedic surgeries for unmatched accuracy and faster recovery.
Regenerative therapies like Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) and stem cell treatments for natural healing.
Rehabilitation programs focused on strength, mobility, and sport-specific performance.
Each treatment plan is personalized, taking into account the athlete’s training schedule, career goals, and long-term joint health.

How long does recovery take? Recovery varies depending on the type of injury and procedure. While minor injuries may take a few weeks, ligament reconstructions or cartilage procedures may require several months of guided rehabilitation.
Is surgery always required? Not every sports injury needs surgery. Many cases can be effectively managed through physiotherapy, regenerative injections, and rehabilitation programs. Surgery is considered only when conservative treatment fails or when the injury threatens long-term performance.
