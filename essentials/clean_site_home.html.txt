Exceptional Care
Dr. Nithin Kumar Bejjanki is an internationally trained orthopaedic surgeon with 24 years of experience, specialising in arthroscopy, sports injuries, complex knee and shoulder conditions, cartilage regeneration, and robotic-assisted surgeries . With advanced training in the UK under globally renowned surgeons, he brings world-class expertise to every patient.
Having successfully treated elite athletes, national players, and complex trauma cases , he is widely respected for his ethical practice, surgical precision, and outstanding outcomes . His care extends from elite professionals to everyday patients, ensuring that everyone benefits from the same high level of dedication and excellence.
With a focus on evidence-based treatments , cutting-edge techniques, and patient-centered care, Dr. Nithin ensures the best possible recovery, faster return to activity, and long-term joint health.
