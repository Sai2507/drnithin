Rotator Cuff Repair

Rotator Cuff Repair is a specialized orthopedic procedure designed to treat tears or injuries in the rotator cuff tendons, which are crucial for shoulder stability and mobility. Rotator cuff injuries often result from trauma, repetitive overhead activity, or degenerative changes due to aging. Under the care of Dr. Nithin Kumar Bejjanki, patients benefit from advanced diagnostic evaluation, minimally invasive arthroscopic techniques, and personalized rehabilitation programs, ensuring effective recovery and restoration of shoulder function.

Rotator Cuff Repair addresses patients with:

  • Shoulder pain, especially when lifting the arm or performing overhead activities

  • Weakness or loss of shoulder strength

  • Limited range of motion or stiffness

  • Pain at night, affecting sleep

  • Partial or full-thickness rotator cuff tears

  • Shoulder injuries from sports, accidents, or degenerative conditions

This service targets both traumatic injuries and chronic degenerative tears, helping patients regain strength, stability, and normal shoulder function.

Non-Surgical / Conservative Approaches:

  • Physical therapy to strengthen surrounding muscles and restore mobility

  • Pain management through anti-inflammatory medications or steroid injections

  • Activity modification to reduce strain and prevent further injury

Surgical / Advanced Interventions:

  • Arthroscopic rotator cuff repair for minimally invasive tendon repair

  • Open surgical repair in complex or large tears

  • Tendon transfer or grafting for irreparable injuries

  • Regenerative therapies like PRP injections to support healing

The choice of treatment depends on tear size, patient age, activity level, and functional goals.

  1. Initial Assessment: Comprehensive history, physical examination, and imaging (MRI or ultrasound)

  2. Surgical Planning: Selecting arthroscopic or open repair technique based on tear severity

  3. Surgical Procedure: Repairing torn tendons using sutures, anchors, or grafts under anesthesia

  4. Closure & Dressing: Small incisions closed and bandaged (for arthroscopic repair)

  5. Rehabilitation: Structured physiotherapy program to restore range of motion, strength, and function

Patients typically experience reduced pain, improved mobility, and gradual return to normal activities within a structured recovery timeline.

Q1: How long does recovery take after rotator cuff repair?
A: Most patients regain daily activity in 3–4 months, while full strength and overhead function may take 6–9 months.

Q2: Can I avoid surgery?
A: Minor or partial tears may improve with physical therapy and conservative treatment, but surgery is recommended for full-thickness tears or persistent pain.

Q3: How soon can I start physiotherapy?
A: Gentle passive exercises usually begin within a week post-surgery, with gradual strengthening over several months.

Q4: Are there risks associated with surgery?
A: Complications are rare but may include infection, stiffness, or re-tear; proper post-op care reduces risk.

Q5: When can I resume sports or heavy activity?
A: Return to sports typically occurs 6–9 months, depending on injury severity, repair type, and adherence to rehabilitation.

Q6: Will shoulder function fully return?
A: Most patients regain significant pain relief, mobility, and strength, enabling safe return to daily and sports activities.
